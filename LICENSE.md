### 1.1 Exception: textures made from Minetest Game textures (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Some generated textures are built on top of Minetest Game textures (the
player skin, wood, stone and the material blocks). As changed versions of
CC BY-SA 3.0 works they stay under **CC BY-SA 3.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), credit: the Minetest
Game contributors, changed by SEL Island. These are:

* all character skins and pictures made from them: `sel_looks_*.png`
  (except `sel_looks_wardrobe.png`, see below), `sel_lake_ferrymaster.png`,
  `sel_irrigation_inventor.png`, `sel_flower_gardener.png`,
  `sel_village_mayor.png`, `sel_village_child.png`,
  `sel_village_grandma.png`, `sel_village_grandpa.png`,
  `sel_village_portrait_*.png`
* `sel_looks_wardrobe.png`, `sel_lake_story_*.png`,
  `sel_irrigation_channel_top.png`, `sel_irrigation_channel_top_wet.png`,
  `sel_village_gem_ore.png`, `sel_village_plank.png`

Everything else in `mods/sel/*/textures` and `mods/sel/*/sounds` is drawn
from scratch by a script and is MIT like the rest.
